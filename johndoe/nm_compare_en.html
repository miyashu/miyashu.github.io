NM and MM.COM (MultiMente): a comparison of the specifications

As of NM (Next Maintenance) v0.99j (evaluation version, 2026-11-22). NM is compared with the manual of MultiMente v2.07 (MM.TXT). NM is newly written and takes over the operation of MM; it uses none of MM's program.

  • 1. The same
  • 2. Added
  • 3. Changed
  • 4. Left out
  • 5. Not yet done

Compatibility with MM's settings: NM reads MM's MMRET.DAT and MMMCR.DAT as they are. Lines of MM.CFG can be copied to NMCFG.DAT (unknown items are skipped). The environment variable %MM% is also used with the same meaning as in MM. MultiMente (including MMRET.DAT and MMMCR.DAT) can be downloaded from the GIGAMIX site (https://www.gigamix.jp/).

How it was made: the references were the MM manual (MM.TXT), the sample settings file (MM.CFG), the format of the DAT files, and the screens and behaviour seen by running MM. The contents of MM's program were not used, and it was not disassembled.

There are five categories.

  1. The same
  2. Added (not in MM)
  3. Changed (the same purpose, but different behaviour or keys)
  4. Left out (decided not to have)
  5. Not yet done (in MM, not yet implemented in NM)

MM's behaviour is taken from what its manual says. Details not written in the manual (such as the result of macro expansion) have not been fully compared.
